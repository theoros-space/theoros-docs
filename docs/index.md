# Theoros

Theoros builds onboard AI for Earth observation satellites. A satellite sees
far more than it can send home, and much of what it captures is cloud. Our
model runs on the satellite, classifies each image as it is captured and sends
down answers instead of raw pixels.

**Who it is for:** operators of Earth observation satellites and the space
agencies and integrators who build them.

**What makes it different:** one model under one million parameters that
adapts to any band set and any flight processor, delivered with everything
needed to fly it.
